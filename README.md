# Credit Card Revenue & Customer Analysis

## Overview

Analyzed credit card transaction and customer data using **MySQL and Power BI**
to understand revenue drivers, customer value, spending behaviour and revenue
trends.

The analysis focuses on a simple business question:

**Where is the revenue coming from, who is driving it, and where can the
business focus its efforts?**

## Key Insights

- **Businessmen are the highest-value profession**, generating ₹17.70M revenue
  and contributing around 31% of total revenue.
- **Customers aged 40–50 generate the highest revenue**, with the 40–50 and
  50–60 segments together contributing roughly 78% of total revenue.
- **Bills is the largest expenditure category**, making recurring everyday
  spending an important revenue driver.
- **Businessmen also generate the highest interest earned**, contributing
  approximately ₹2.58M.
- Revenue shows **significant weekly fluctuations**, highlighting the need
  to monitor performance over time rather than relying only on overall totals.
- The analysis also identifies **high-value customers and revenue
  concentration**, providing a basis for targeted customer strategies.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus retention and rewards strategies on the **40–60 age segment**, which
  contributes the largest share of revenue.
- Develop targeted offers and premium benefits for **Businessman customers**,
  the strongest professional segment.
- Encourage repeat card usage around **high-value everyday spending categories**
  such as bill payments.
- Combine customer attributes such as **age, profession, card category and
  spending behaviour** to identify more precise targeting opportunities.
- Use monthly and quarterly revenue trends to **time campaigns and monitor
  changes in business performance**.

## Tools & Skills

**MySQL:** SQL analysis, joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs, CASE statements,
window functions, ranking and time-based analysis.

**Power BI:** Data modeling, Power Query, DAX, KPI analysis, dashboard design
and business reporting.

**Excel:** Data preparation and initial analysis.
